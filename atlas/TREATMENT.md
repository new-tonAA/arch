# 《建筑年表》 / A Timeline of Buildings

**6:00 · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 72 buildings · 4,500 years · no narration**

A year-driven montage, not an era essay. Each plate is 4.5 s: a giant year,
one building, its city and typology, and a running timeline. The visual
language changes by era, but the layout never does — the film is one long
index that keeps accelerating.

## 1. Three structures

**A. 72 plates, one year each (chosen).** 72 buildings from 前 2560 to 2025,
each 4.5 s, cut on a 120 BPM grid. The year is the hero; the building is the
answer. The reference is the density of a 98-film Oscar montage in 5–6 min:
information arrives faster than a documentary, but every card is legible.

**B. Seven era chapters.** The previous film's structure: 12–15 s per era,
one building per chapter. It reads as an essay; the years and buildings are
too sparse.

**C. One continuous scroll.** A single 4,500-year timeline with buildings
passing the camera. Elegant, but the silhouettes race by without time to
register the year.

**Choice A** because the brief was explicit: the year and *what appeared
that year* must be the subject, and the pace must be compact.

## 2. Logline and shape

**Logline** 4,500 years of building, compressed into 72 plates — every year
a new answer to the same question: how do we live together?

- **0:00–0:08** a year counter spins from 2026 back to 前 2600; the title and
  the 72-bar index appear.
- **0:08–5:32** 72 plates, 4.5 s each. The timeline rail fills; the year
  jumps across centuries as the buildings demand.
- **5:32–6:00** all 72 years reassemble into one wall; the title returns;
  the timeline is still running.

## 4. Plate grammar (every 4.5 s)

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–0.4 | year card wipes in; the year delta appears |
| 2–5 | 0.4–2.7 | building draws/uncovered; camera pushes 2.5 % |
| 6–8 | 2.7–4.2 | tag + city hold; rail dot lands; motif note |
| 9 | 4.2–4.5 | page wipe / hard cut; next year |

The layout is fixed: year and name on the left (0–560 px), building on the
right (900–1850 px), era label above, timeline rail at the bottom.

## 5. Seven languages

| Era | Plates | Language | Background / type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 古代 | 8 | copperplate line + hatching | cream paper, Source Han Serif |
| 中世纪 | 8 | lapis, gold, ruby flat glass | near-black, gold serif |
| 文艺复兴与巴洛克 | 8 | pen + transparent wash | paper, KaiTi / Georgia |
| 工业 | 9 | cyanotype blueprint | Prussian blue, white Consolas |
| 现代主义 | 15 | Swiss white/ink/red | 12-column grid, Bahnschrift |
| 后现代 | 10 | Memphis pastel collage | cream, Arial Black |
| 当代 | 14 | LiDAR point cloud / HUD | near-black, cyan Consolas |

The transition at each era boundary is a **material change**: ink floods the
paper, gold drains to wash, a blueprint sheet slides over the drawing, the
blueprint bleaches to Swiss white, a knife cuts the grid into pastel shapes,
and the pastel shatters into points.

## 6. Camera and rhythm

- No camera travel inside a plate; a 2–3 % push and a ±26 px lateral drift.
- Most plates reveal from the ground up; about a quarter are uncovered from
  the left. This keeps the cuts from feeling like a slideshow.
- Hard cuts on the 9-beat grid; the only long transitions are the six era
  boundaries (0.7 s material wipes).
- The film accelerates by **density**, not by shortening the plates: ancient
  plates are sparse, modern plates carry more window/floor detail, and the
  final wall of 72 years is the release.

## 7. Sound

120 BPM; each plate is 9 beats. One kick + one woodblock tick on the
downbeat, bass on beats 1 and 3, a snare on 2 and 4, hats on eighths. The
era changes the palette:

| Era | Instruments |
|---|---|
| 古代 | frame drum, plucked lyre, stone ticks |
| 中世纪 | organ, bells, chant-like pad |
| 文艺复兴 | harpsichord, lute |
| 工业 | anvil, steam, brass, sub pulse |
| 现代主义 | marimba, vibraphone, dry clicks |
| 后现代 | pizzicato, pops, woodblock |
| 当代 | granular pad, sonar pings, sub |
| 终章 | full motif, bells, final chord |

A 7-note motif (D–A–D–E–F–E–D) is played every two plates, transposed by era.
There is **no narration**: the year cards carry the information, like the
reference montage.

## 8. Facts and rights

`FACTS.md` lists all 72 buildings with a date and a source link. Dates are
completion dates unless marked 约; contested cases are noted. Every building
is a simplified diagram drawn in code, not a photograph or a copied drawing.
Modern buildings remain their architects' intellectual property; the film
makes no endorsement claim.
